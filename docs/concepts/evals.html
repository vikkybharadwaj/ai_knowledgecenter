Concepts · evals & reliability

How do you know it actually works?

An AI product’s quality isn’t a feeling; it’s a measurement — and the measurement is a machine that can break, drift and lie. This page is ten mental models for building evals you can trust, one picture of how they fit together, and twelve lessons from rebuilding a real eval system. Read it top to bottom; every idea opens in place.

The whole picture

Evals are a loop, not a one-off test. You find what goes wrong, turn each failure into a check, run the checks forever — and let what production teaches you feed the next round. Click any idea to jump to it.
