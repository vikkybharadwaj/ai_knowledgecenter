Concepts · reference

Legend — what the badges mean

Every primitive page opens with a row of badges, like the one on Policies:

Governance Hard · client-enforced GA

They read left to right as three independent axes: where it sits (altitude) · who’s in control (enforcement) · how finished it is (maturity). Extra plain badges may tack on a version or a spec date.

AXIS 1

Altitude — how much coordination

The first, colored badge. It marks the primitive’s grouping — the same three altitudes the rest of the site uses. Colour = altitude; the word may vary by primitive.

Foundations

Drive one session well — the soft context that steers a single agentic loop.

Extend

Extend a session — reusable know-how, external reach, guaranteed behaviour, and the first taste of parallelism.

Orchestrate Governance

Orchestrate many agents & fleet governance. Governance is the same altitude, used when the primitive is about control rather than coordination (e.g. policies).

AXIS 2

Enforcement — who’s in control

The middle badge. Does the client guarantee the behaviour, or does the model choose to follow it?

Hard · client-enforced Hard · deterministic

Hard. Runs or blocks regardless of what the model decides — hooks, permission rules, managed policies. The escape hatch from “the model usually complies.”

Soft · model-mediated

Soft. Guidance the model reads and usually follows, with no compliance guarantee — CLAUDE.md, skills, output styles.

Tools model-invoked Coordinated by the main session

Plain (uncoloured) badges in this slot just describe the mechanism — who calls it or how it’s wired — when “hard / soft” isn’t the useful distinction.

AXIS 3

Maturity — how finished it is

The availability badge. How much should you trust it to stay put?

GA

Generally available. Stable and documented; safe to build on.

preview research preview

Preview. Usable but still moving — shapes and flags may change between releases.

experimental

Experimental. Often off by default; expect rough edges and breaking changes.

v2.1.139+ open standard · 2024-11-25

Plain trailing badges pin a version (the build a feature landed in) or a spec date. Datestamps are deliberate — models & previews go stale fast.
