About the exam

60 Qs

scenario-based multiple choice

120 min

no negative marking

720 / 1000

scaled passing score

6 scenarios

4 drawn per sitting (community-reported)

How to study Read the six scenarios until the “situation → fix” mapping is automatic, work the practice quiz by domain (read every explanation — the exam rewards knowing why the other three options are wrong), then take a full exam-mode run to simulate the 60-question / 720-to-pass experience. Your weak scenario shows up as a weak domain. The underlying material lives in Concepts.
