Concepts · evals & reliability

How do you know it actually works?

An AI product’s quality isn’t a feeling; it’s a measurement — and the measurement is a machine that can break, drift and lie. This page is ten mental models for building evals you can trust, one picture of how they fit together, and lessons from rebuilding a real eval system. Read it top to bottom; every idea opens in place.

The whole picture

Evals are a loop, not a test suite you write once: find what goes wrong, grade it honestly, fix each cause where it lives, prove the fix — then automate, and go round again. Each step links to the mental models (M) and real-build lessons (L) that explain it. Prefer prose? Read the whole loop as one note.
